About

Simulation that works
for everyone

Syncraft was founded on a simple belief: powerful manufacturing simulation should not be reserved for enterprises with seven-figure software budgets. We build accessible, intuitive tools that let engineers and planners model, test, and optimise production systems — without the complexity or cost of legacy platforms.

Built from the ground up in Denmark, our platform combines years of industrial know-how with modern software thinking. From discrete-event simulation to real-time dashboards, Syncraft gives your team the insight to make better decisions, faster.
